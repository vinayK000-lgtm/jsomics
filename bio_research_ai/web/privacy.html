Privacy Policy

Information Processed

The JSOMICS Custom GPT action may send research questions, disease names, gene or protein names, pathway names, drug names, research context, and optional evidence snippets to the JSOMICS API.

How Information Is Used

Submitted information is used to retrieve matching evidence and return structured research outputs including biomarkers, pathways, literature findings, drug targets, caveats, references, and provenance.

Do Not Submit Patient Data

Do not submit protected health information, patient identifiers, individual genetic test results, confidential clinical records, or information you are not authorized to process.

Research-Use Limitation

JSOMICS outputs are for research use only. Clinical decisions require physician review and validated diagnostic tests.

Contact

For privacy or data-processing questions, contact the operator of the JSOMICS deployment.
